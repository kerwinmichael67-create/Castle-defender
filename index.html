Castle Defender Online

Hold the wall · Sell the dead

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Man a crossbow on the wall and shoot the horde. Kills leave bodies: drag them to the pile and sell them.
  2. Walk up to the sergeant (barracks, east of the gate), the blacksmith (armoury, west) or the steward (inside the keep at the back) and press E: their panel opens beside you and the number keys buy.
  3. The steward sells castle works; the master-at-arms in the training yard drills your people into veterans; the blacksmith also changes your class: swordsman, archer or spearman.
  4. Climb the corner towers from the wall walk, pray at the chapel, drink at the well, borrow a horse at the stables, try the fountain, sit the throne, plan defences at the war table, feast in the hall, sharpen at the grindstone, sleep in a bunk, heave powder kegs off the wall, light the tower beacons.
  5. Press Q at a crossbow for its 5 upgrades, then rebuild it as a rapid-fire crossbow, ballista or cannon.
  6. Hire 3 engineers to crew the great ballista on the gate tower, climb the ladder to command it, and evolve it into a hwacha, trebuchet or bombard.
  7. The war table in the keep is a map of the field: mark spikes, snares, tar pits and barricades, and hired builders go out to build and upgrade them. Every 25th wave brings the Dread Titan.
  8. Each corner tower carries a heavy tower cannon on its lookout: 10 levels, then one of three evolutions — thunder mortar, dragonfire cannon or organ gun.
  9. Royal seals buy lasting boons and banner colours at the Royal Charter on this menu. In the keep, the steward's Grand works and Royal favours take gold and seals too.

CONTROLS

  • WASD move · mouse look · Shift sprint
  • E use: crossbow, ladder, body, cauldron — hold at the market to sell
  • Click fire, or swing your sword on foot · Right-click zoom
  • E trade with the sergeant, blacksmith or steward · Q weapon stats · U upgrade it
  • F arrow volley · R war horn · G hold to aim a fire pot, release to throw · B toll the chapel bell
  • H hide or show the garrison list
  • M free or capture the mouse (it frees itself when a panel opens) · X mute · O graphics: high, medium, low, performance
  • T aim mode: hold, or toggle (click once to stay zoomed, again to stop)
  • 8 repair the castle · N next wave · Esc pause and save code
Each chest holds one castle skin. Every skin is its own design — a different keep, towers, battlements and trees — standing on the same spots.
CROSSBOW SKINS
Crossbow skins are bought outright with seals. They refit every crossbow, ballista and cannon on the wall.
Seals: 1 for every wave you clear, 3 for a blood moon wave. They are kept between matches in this browser.
Lasting boons for every match, bought with royal seals
BANNER COLOURS
Boons last for good in this browser. The steward in the keep also trades in seals: tribute, masons, gold and drillmasters.
